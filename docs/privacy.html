Privacy Policy — RomCloud

Updated: October 6, 2026. Contact: bun2it@gmail.com

1. Data we access

  • Google account (email, via OAuth): only to link and display the correct account in the app.
  • Google Drive (drive.file scope): the app only reads/writes files created by RomCloud itself or files you explicitly open/share (ROMs, backups, calendar). It does not read your entire Drive and does not delete files outside this scope.
  • Device: anonymous device identifier and technical crash logs used to fix bugs. No ads, no sale of data.

2. Storage

Login tokens are stored encrypted locally on your device. Our servers do not store your Drive files or tokens; anonymous crash reports are stored on GitHub Issues (Romcloud_Logs repository).

3. Sharing

We do not share personal data with third parties, except public Google/Microsoft APIs when you command a sync or play content.

4. Revoking access

You can unlink at any time: in the app (Sign out) or at myaccount.google.com/permissions by removing RomCloud access.
