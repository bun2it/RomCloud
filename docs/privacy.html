Privacy Policy

Updated: October 8, 2026.
Contact: bun2it@gmail.com

This policy describes what data the apps RomCloud (game library manager for the TrimUI Brick handheld) and BrickDrop (offline file sharing between your own devices) collect, why they collect it, and what your choices are. It also covers this website.

1. Data RomCloud collects and why

  • Google account email address — obtained through Google OAuth 2.0. Used only to show which account is linked inside the app. We do not read your Gmail or contacts.
  • Google Drive files — RomCloud requests the drive.file scope (in some setups the broader drive scope). In practice the app only touches files it created itself or files you explicitly open or share with it: your game ROMs, save-data backups, and calendar data. It cannot browse or read the rest of your Drive, and it never deletes files you did not ask it to manage.
  • OAuth tokens (access token, refresh token, expiry time) — stored only in the app's on-device SQLite database (data/library.db, settings table) on your SD card. Tokens are never uploaded to our servers and never written to log files.
  • Device identifier — a value like RC-xxxxxxxx, generated as a SHA-256 hash on first run. It cannot be reversed into your hardware serial number.
  • Crash and error reports — when the app encounters an error, it may send: app version, device model, OS version, display resolution, free/total RAM and storage, network status, and the error message. This is used solely to diagnose and fix bugs.
  • GitHub personal access token — only if you choose to enter one (to enable automatic issue logging). Stored in config/github_token or the app settings on your device.

2. How Google sign-in works on a handheld

The TrimUI Brick has no web browser or keyboard, so RomCloud uses the OAuth 2.0 Device Authorization Flow: the handheld shows a QR code and a user code, you scan it with your phone and approve access there, and the handheld then receives its tokens directly from Google. Your Google password is never typed into — or seen by — the app.

3. Crash reports in detail

Error reports are sent through our relay (romcloud-issue-relay.bun2it.workers.dev) into a private GitHub repository (bun2it/Romcloud_Logs) that only the developer can read. Reports contain the technical details listed in section 1 and never contain your OAuth tokens, Google password, or Drive file contents.

4. Data we do not collect

  • No advertising identifiers and no ads.
  • No analytics SDKs or behavioral tracking.
  • No location data, no contacts, no microphone or camera data.
  • We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it with data brokers.

5. Where your data lives

  • On your device (SD card): OAuth tokens, app settings, your game library database.
  • On Google's servers, under your own account: the Drive files you choose to sync.
  • In a private GitHub repository: anonymized crash reports (see section 3).

We operate no user-account servers of our own; there is no RomCloud cloud account holding your data.

6. Sharing with third parties

Personal data is shared only as needed to provide features you trigger:

  • Google APIs — when you sign in or sync with Google Drive.
  • GitHub — when a crash report is filed to the private logs repository.

We do not share data with anyone else, and we never sell it.

7. Data retention

  • OAuth tokens and settings remain on your device until you sign out or delete the app data.
  • Crash reports are kept only as long as needed to investigate and fix the reported bug.
  • You can ask us to delete crash reports tied to your device identifier at any time via the contact email above.

8. Your choices

  • Sign out inside the RomCloud app at any time — this deletes the stored OAuth tokens from the device.
  • Revoke Google access at myaccount.google.com/permissions by removing RomCloud.
  • Delete local data by removing the app's data folder on your SD card (/mnt/SDCARD/Apps/RomCloud/data and config).
  • Disable crash reporting — remove the GitHub token / turn off issue logging in settings; the app keeps working.

9. BrickDrop — offline by design

BrickDrop sends files directly between your own devices over your local Wi-Fi hotspot or LAN using the LocalSend protocol. There are no user accounts, nothing is uploaded to any cloud server, and our servers never see your files. Nearby-device discovery uses local-network broadcasts so your devices can find each other; no personal data leaves your network.

10. This website

This site is a set of static pages hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not set cookies, run analytics, or collect information from website visitors. If you email us, we use your message only to respond.

11. Children

Our apps are general-audience utilities and are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children; if you believe a child has provided personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the apps evolve; the updated date at the top will change, and material changes will be noted on this site.
